Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a private property viewing?

You can schedule a private tour by clicking "View Details" on any property card and submitting the contact form directly to the listing agent, or by calling our office line at +1 (800) 555-REAL.

Are there fees for listing my property with RealEstate?

Our brokerage operates on a success-based commission model negotiated individually per property listing. There are no upfront hidden listing fees for standard representation.

Do you assist international property buyers?

Yes, over 30% of our buyers are international investors. We provide full support including cross-border tax advisory, currency wire coordination, legal escrow representation, and property management.
